# Trust and Safety Policy

SlabX reviews are tied to completed transactions. Only the buyer and seller of an order with confirmed delivery may review one another, and each participant may submit one review per order.

To reduce retaliatory reviewing, a submitted review remains private until both participants submit or 14 days pass. Public displays do not identify the review author and expose no order, address, payment, or shipment details. Hidden reviews do not contribute to reputation totals.

Signed-in users may report users, listings, and reviews for fraud, counterfeit concerns, harassment, spam, privacy violations, or other policy concerns. Reports are private to authorized moderators. Moderator decisions are role-protected and recorded in immutable moderation and audit logs.

Moderators may assign, hide or restore reviews, resolve, or dismiss reports. A report is an allegation, not proof; moderation should use consistent evidence, document the decision, minimize personal-data exposure, and escalate credible safety, fraud, or legal issues.
