CUSTOMER CARE

Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

Effective August 10, 2026

Pre-launch notice: SlabX does not currently accept live purchases. This policy will apply after launch and may be refined before then.

Before payment

A buyer may cancel a pending offer while it is still awaiting the seller’s response. Leaving or cancelling checkout before payment confirmation does not complete a purchase.

After payment

Because collectible cards are unique and condition-sensitive, completed sales are generally final. A buyer may request help when the item is materially different from its listing, is the wrong item, arrives damaged, or is reasonably suspected to be counterfeit.

Report a problem

Contact support@slabxmarket.com within three calendar days after recorded delivery. Include the order number, a description of the problem, clear photographs of the card and packaging, and any other requested evidence. Keep all original packaging while the case is reviewed.

Approved returns

Do not return an item without instructions. If a return is approved, ship it using the provided method within seven calendar days and preserve its condition. A refund may be reduced or denied if the returned item is different, altered, damaged after delivery, or missing components.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after the return is received or when SlabX determines a return is unnecessary. Bank processing times vary. Original or return shipping treatment will be disclosed when the case is resolved.

Seller cancellations

A seller who cannot fulfill an order must notify SlabX immediately. Repeated cancellations, non-shipment, or inaccurate listings may result in restrictions.

Payment disputes

Please contact SlabX before filing a chargeback so we can investigate. This does not limit any rights available through your card issuer or applicable law.
