Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Pre-launch notice: SlabX is currently under development. This policy describes the data expected for the U.S. marketplace and will be updated before live commerce.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use SlabX, we may collect account and profile details, contact information, shipping addresses, collection and listing information, images, offers, order records, support messages, device information, security logs, and service usage data.

Payments and seller verification

Stripe processes payments and seller identity, banking, and payout information. SlabX does not store complete card or bank-account numbers. Stripe handles this information under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and marketplace features, process and reconcile transactions, prevent fraud, secure the service, communicate about activity, provide support, meet legal obligations, and improve SlabX.

How we share information

We share information only as needed with transaction participants, service providers such as Stripe, hosting, email, and image providers, professional advisers, authorities when legally required, or a successor in a business transaction. We do not sell personal information for money.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for the service, security, financial records, dispute handling, and legal obligations. We use administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may update account information and request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some transaction, security, and legal records must be retained. Email requests to privacy@slabxmarket.com.

Children

SlabX is not intended for children under 18 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the marketplace and legal requirements develop. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date.
